What really happens when you delete your account

Not a deactivation, not a soft delete. A look at what we remove, and the one short window we can’t avoid.

30 Sep 20263 min readFinzyy team

“Delete account” buttons don’t always do what they say. Some only hide your profile; some keep your data “just in case”. We wanted Finzyy’s to be exactly what it sounds like.

What we remove

When you confirm deletion in the app (Account→Privacy & legal→Delete account), we permanently delete:

  • your sign-in: mobile number, email, password hash, and the Google or phone sign-in record held by our sign-in provider;
  • your profile details;
  • every transaction and the notes on them;
  • your budgets, alert history and the merchant categories you taught the app;
  • the notification token for your phone;
  • any family group you created that nobody else joined.

The one window we can’t avoid

Our database provider keeps a short rolling recovery history so the service can be restored after an outage. Deleted data can stay in that history for up to 6 hours, and then it’s gone for good. We don’t keep a copy, an archive, or an “inactive” version of your account.

Because nothing is kept, deletion can’t be undone. If you sign in again later with the same number, you start with a fresh, empty account.

No app? Email works too

If you’ve uninstalled Finzyy, you can ask us to delete your account by email — see the account deletion page. We’ll confirm it’s you, delete everything listed above, and reply within 7 business days.

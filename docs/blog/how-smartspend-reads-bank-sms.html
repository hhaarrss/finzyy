How SmartSpend reads your bank SMS without sending them anywhere

The message stays on your phone. Here’s exactly what happens between your bank’s alert and the line in your ledger.

30 Sep 20265 min readSmartSpend team

Most of us already get a text for every payment: a UPI transfer, a card swipe, a salary credit. Those messages are a complete record of where money goes — they’re just scattered across an inbox. SmartSpend’s job is to gather them into one ledger without asking you to type anything, and without the messages themselves ever leaving your phone.

1. Only bank messages get a look

When an SMS arrives, the first thing SmartSpend checks is who sent it. Indian banks and payment apps send alerts from short sender IDs (the HDFCBK or ICICIB you see at the top of the message). If the sender isn’t on SmartSpend’s list of known bank and payment senders, the message is ignored right there — your conversations, delivery updates and OTPs from other apps never go any further.

2. The details are picked out on your phone

For a bank alert, the app reads the text on the device and picks out just the parts that describe the payment:

This is a simplified example — every bank words its alerts differently, and not every alert carries every field. Everything outside those fields — including the wording of the message and the phone numbers banks print in them — stays on the phone.

3. Only the details travel, encrypted

Those fields are sent over an encrypted connection to your account. On our side, the payment is categorised (Food & Dining, Transportation…), checked against your earlier payments so the same SMS can never be counted twice, and added to your totals, budgets and trends.

If you’re offline when a payment comes in, the details wait on your phone — encrypted with a key kept in the phone’s secure keystore — and sync the moment you’re back online. The app also checks your inbox in the background for bank alerts it might have missed.

What if I’d rather not share SMS at all?

That’s fine. SMS access is optional; without it, you add payments by hand and every other feature works the same. You can turn it off at any time from your phone’s settings. The full detail is in our privacy policy.

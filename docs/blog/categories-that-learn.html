Fix a category once, and never again

Why a payment sometimes lands in the wrong category, and how one correction teaches Finzyy for good.

30 Sep 20263 min readFinzyy team

Finzyy files every payment under a category so your spending adds up into something meaningful. Most of the time it gets this right on its own — Uber is transport, Swiggy is food. But bank alerts don’t always name a shop the way you’d expect.

Why a payment can land in the wrong place

A UPI payment to a small shop might arrive as a personal UPI handle or a shortened business name that tells us nothing about what you bought. When Finzyy can’t tell, it doesn’t guess: the payment is marked as needing a category, so it doesn’t quietly skew your totals.

Fixing it takes one tap — right from the notification

When a payment syncs, Finzyy shows a notification with the amount and merchant. If it needs a category, tap Categorize and pick one from the sheet that opens over whatever you’re doing — no need to open the app. You can also change the category of any payment from the transaction itself.

And it’s remembered

A correction doesn’t just fix one payment. Finzyy remembers that this merchant belongs in that category for you, and applies it to every future payment there automatically. Your choice always wins over the app’s own guesses — if you say your gym is Personal Care rather than Healthcare, that’s where it goes from now on.

Corrections are personal: they apply to your account only, and they’re deleted along with everything else if you delete your account.

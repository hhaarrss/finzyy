What it does

Everything a ledger should do, nothing it shouldn’t.

01

Automatic from SMS

Bank and UPI alerts become transactions the moment they arrive — even ones that came in while your phone was offline.

02

Categories that learn

Fix a merchant’s category once, straight from the notification, and every future payment there is filed the same way.

03

Budgets that warn early

Set a monthly budget overall or per category. You’re told as you approach it, not after you’ve gone over.

04

Trends at a glance

See spending by week or month, by category or by merchant, and how this month compares with the last.

How it works

From bank SMS to ledger, in three steps.

The message itself never leaves your phone. Only the payment details do.

Step 1

Your bank sends an SMS

SmartSpend checks who it’s from. Anything that isn’t a known bank or payment sender is ignored — personal chats and OTPs included.

Step 2

Read on your phone

The app picks out the payment details on the device itself.

amount · debit/credit · merchant · date · bank · last 4 digits · UPI ref
Step 3

Filed and categorised

Only those details are sent, encrypted, to your account, where the payment is categorised and added to your totals and budgets.

Privacy

Built so your messages stay yours.

What we do

  • Read bank SMS on your phone and send only the payment details
  • Encrypt everything between the app and our server
  • Encrypt your sign-in on the phone with its secure keystore
  • Delete your account and all its data when you ask — in the app or by email

What we never do

  • Upload or store the text of any SMS
  • Read personal conversations or OTPs
  • Sell your data or use it for advertising
  • Run analytics or advertising trackers in the app

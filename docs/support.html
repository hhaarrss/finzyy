Getting started

Why does SmartSpend need SMS access?

To turn the payment alerts your bank sends into transactions automatically. SmartSpend only looks at messages from known bank and payment senders; everything else is ignored. It’s optional — without it you can still add payments by hand. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Which banks are supported?

SmartSpend recognises payment alerts from these senders. Message formats vary between banks and change over time; if a payment is missed, add it by hand and tell us so we can support that format.

Can I use SmartSpend without giving SMS permission?

Yes. Use Add on the Home screen to enter payments yourself — budgets, trends and categories all work the same.

Privacy & data

Is my SMS data sent to your servers?

No. SMS messages are read on your phone. Only the extracted payment details — amount, debit or credit, merchant, date, bank, the last 4 digits of the account and the UPI reference — are sent to your account, encrypted. The original message text is never uploaded or stored. The category is worked out afterwards, from those details.

Do you read my personal messages or OTPs?

No. Messages that aren’t from a known bank or payment sender are ignored before anything is read into the app.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Account→Privacy & legal→Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. If you no longer have the app, use our account deletion page.

Using the app

A transaction was categorized incorrectly — how do I fix it?

Open the transaction and pick the right category, or tap Categorize on the notification when the payment arrives. Your correction is remembered, so future payments to the same merchant are filed the same way automatically.

A payment from my bank didn’t show up.

Check that SmartSpend is allowed to read SMS in your phone’s app settings. Payments that arrive while you’re offline sync as soon as you’re back online. If it still doesn’t appear, add it by hand and email us the bank name so we can look at that message format — please don’t send us the SMS text itself.

I’m not getting notifications.

Allow notifications for SmartSpend in your phone’s settings, and check the Transaction alerts channel isn’t muted. Budget alerts can be switched on or off in Account.

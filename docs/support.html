Frequently asked questions

Why does Finzyy need SMS access?

Finzyy reads SMS messages only from your bank's official sender IDs (e.g. HDFCBK, ICICIB) to automatically detect and log your transactions. This is entirely optional — you can use the app with manual entry only. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Is my SMS data sent to your servers?

No. SMS parsing happens entirely on your device. Only the extracted transaction details (amount, merchant, category, date) are sent to our backend — never the original message text.

A transaction was categorized incorrectly — how do I fix it?

Open the transaction and tap to recategorize it. Your correction is remembered, so future transactions from the same merchant will be categorized correctly automatically.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Profile → Privacy & Data → Delete My Account within the app. If you no longer have the app installed, visit our account deletion page.

Which banks are supported?

Finzyy currently supports ICICI, HDFC, SBI, and Axis Bank SMS formats, with more being added.

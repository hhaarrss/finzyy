Delete Your Finzyy Account

If you still have the app installed, the fastest way to delete your account is directly in-app: Profile → Privacy & Data → Delete My Account. This immediately and permanently removes your account and all associated data.

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion from here instead.

What gets deleted
  • Your account and login information
  • All transaction records
  • All budget limits and categorization history

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

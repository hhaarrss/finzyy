Remove your SmartSpend account and everything in it — in the app, or by email if you no longer have it.

01Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open SmartSpend and tap your profile picture on the Home screen.
  2. Go to Account→Privacy & legal→Delete account
  3. Type DELETE to confirm (and enter your password if your account has one). Your account and data are deleted straight away.

02What gets deleted

Sign-inMobile number, email, password (if you set one), and your Google or phone sign-in record
ProfileName, date of birth, gender, city, occupation and monthly income
TransactionsAll your transactions and the notes on them
Budgets & learningBudgets, budget alert history and the merchant categories you corrected
DeviceYour device’s notification token
Family groupsGroups you created that nobody else has joined

We don’t keep a copy. Deleted data can remain in our database provider’s short-term recovery history for up to 6 hours, after which it is gone for good. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

03No longer have the app? Request by email

Email us with the subject “Account Deletion Request”. Send it from the email address on your account, or — if you signed in with your mobile number — include that number in the message. If we can’t match the request to your account, we’ll reply to confirm it’s you before deleting anything.

Request deletion by email

We’ll delete your account and confirm by email within 7 business days.

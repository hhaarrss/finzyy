Delete Your SmartSpend Account

If you still have the SmartSpend app installed, the fastest way is in the app: tap your profile picture on the Home screen to open Account → Privacy & legal → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm (and enter your password if your account has one). Your account and data are deleted straight away.

If you no longer have the app, you can ask us to delete your account instead — see below.

What gets deleted
  • Your account and sign-in details: mobile number, email, password (if you set one), and your Google or phone sign-in record
  • Your profile: name, date of birth, gender, city, occupation and monthly income
  • All your transactions and the notes on them
  • Your budgets, budget alert history and the merchant categories you corrected
  • Your device’s notification token
  • Family groups you created that nobody else has joined

We don’t keep a copy. Deleted data can remain in our database provider’s short-term recovery history for up to 6 hours, after which it is gone for good. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

Email us with the subject “Account Deletion Request”. Send it from the email address on your account, or — if you signed in with your mobile number — include that number in the message. If we can’t match the request to your account, we’ll reply to confirm it’s you before deleting anything.

Email to request deletion

We will delete your account and confirm by email within 7 business days. See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your data.
